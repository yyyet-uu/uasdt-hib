❓ Frequently asked
How do I earn points?
Watch available ads, complete verified community tasks, claim streak rewards, complete missions and invite active users.
How do referrals work?
Your referral link connects new users to your team. Referral bonuses are released after required activity is verified by the server.
Are arcade games using my balance?
No. The arcade is designed as free-to-play. The server determines each game result and any reward.
Why can an ad reward differ?
Active bonus events, VIP multipliers, first-ad rewards, combos and other server-side rules can affect the final reward.
Where are payment proofs?
Successful payouts are published in the official payment proof channel linked from the wallet.
