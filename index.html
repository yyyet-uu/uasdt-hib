Common questions
How do I earn?
Watch available ads, complete missions and tasks, keep your streak alive, and invite active users through your referral link.
When are referral rewards credited?
Referral rewards depend on verified activity. A referral must meet the required conditions before rewards are credited.
What network is used for withdrawals?
Withdrawals use USDT on BEP20 / BNB Smart Chain. Double-check the address before submitting.
What is the minimum withdrawal?
The current app configuration uses 10,000 PTS as the minimum withdrawal threshold.
Why do I need channel verification?
Required channel membership is part of the app's account-unlock and eligibility flow.

USDT Hub will never need your seed phrase, private key or wallet password. If anyone asks for them, do not send them.
